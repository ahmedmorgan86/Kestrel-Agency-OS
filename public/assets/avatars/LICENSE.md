# Human avatar models

These four rigged GLB models are bundled from Tevy's pinned v1 releases under CC0 1.0:

- Casual Walker: https://tevy.dev/models/casual-walker
- Dog Walker: https://tevy.dev/models/dog-walker
- Hiker: https://tevy.dev/models/hiker
- Road Worker: https://tevy.dev/models/road-worker

The downloaded files use LOD1. Each model includes a 56-bone humanoid rig and looping `idle`, `walk`, `wave`, and `look_around` animations. The avatars are virtual representations and are reused with deterministic appearance variants for the 30-person sample roster.
