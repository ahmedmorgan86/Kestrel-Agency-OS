The 3D colleague models in this folder were generated with Ara 3D Gen
(https://github.com/TuriaArt/3d-gen-TU) from MakeHuman character assets.

The MakeHuman base mesh, morph targets, skeleton, skins, eyes, hair and clothing
assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The exported GLB models were optimized for web delivery (WebP textures and
Meshopt geometry compression). They may be used in commercial and
non-commercial projects without attribution.
